(01) How it works

A gift box,
one step at a time.

yourstore.com/build-your-own
  1. 01

    Choose a box, fill it

    Shoppers pick a box, give it a name ("Sam's birthday box") and fill it from your product steps. Products fly into the box as they're added and the total counts up in a panel that follows them down the page.

  2. 02

    Add a card

    A card step lets them choose a design and write a personal message, or leave it blank for a handwritten note.

  3. 03

    Review everything

    Every step on one page, each with its own edit link, and a clear breakdown of the box, items and card.

  4. 04

    Close the lid

    The whole box goes into the cart in one go. The lid closes with a burst of confetti, and the box name follows the order all the way to your packing slip.

(04) FAQ

Good
questions.

Can I use it with a page builder?

Yes. Place the [bundle_builder], [bb_checkout_cards] and [bb_cart_icon] shortcodes in any shortcode or code module.

Where do card designs come from?

From a product category you choose in the settings. Every published, in-stock simple product in it is offered as a card design, in menu order.

Does it work with the block Cart and Checkout?

Bundles, cards and the mini cart work with them. Nesting cards under their product on the cart page and the checkout card section need the classic (shortcode) Cart and Checkout pages.

Does it reduce the stock of products inside a bundle?

When you turn on "Reduce the stock of every item inside a bundle" in the settings. The box itself always has its stock reduced, like any product.

Will my theme break it?

The builder adapts to the width it's given, whether that's full width, beside a sidebar or in a page builder column. The "Your box" panel keeps floating even in themes that break sticky positioning, and every template can be overridden from your theme.
